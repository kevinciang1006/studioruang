Project 02

Joo Chiat Shophouse

Photo — full-bleed hero
front room toward courtyard
CategoryResidential · Heritage
Year2024
LocationJoo Chiat, Singapore
ScopeInterior architecture, FF&E, styling

A conservation shophouse returned to light, without erasing its age.

The house had been subdivided twice since the 1970s, leaving a dark spine down its centre. Our first move was subtraction: removing the later partitions, reopening the airwell, and letting the plan read end to end again.

The original terrazzo was lifted, cleaned and relaid where it survived; where it did not, a warm lime screed takes its place so the repair is legible rather than disguised. New joinery in stained oak is kept low and continuous, running behind the heritage elements instead of competing with them.

Upstairs, three bedrooms were reduced to two, with a shared reading landing at the airwell. The family wanted somewhere to sit that was neither a bedroom nor the living room; that landing has become the most used room in the house.
